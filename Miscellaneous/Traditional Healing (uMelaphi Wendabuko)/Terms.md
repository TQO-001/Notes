Here’s a **reorganized set of tables** by category, with Zulu terms, translations, and explanations for each distinct aspect of Sangoma practices:

---

### **1. Sangoma Events**  
| **Zulu Term**     | **English Translation**      | **Description**                                                                 |
|--------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| uGugeniswa         | Invitation to Water          | First initiation step involving water rituals to accept ancestral calling.      |
| uThwasa            | Initiation Process           | Formal training period (months/years) to become a Sangoma.                      |
| Impande            | Root Stage                   | Deep ancestral connection rituals; involves secrecy and animal sacrifice.       |
| Phothularing       | Establishing Sacred Space    | Final stage where a Sangoma creates their *sgodlo* (healing space).             |
| uMngcwabo          | Funeral Ritual               | Ceremony to honor ancestors and guide the deceased’s spirit.                    |

---

### **2. Sangoma Tools**  
| **Zulu Term**     | **English Translation**      | **Description**                                                                 |
|--------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Ishoba             | Cow Tail Stick               | Sweeps away negative energy or blesses clients.                                 |
| Izinkalo           | Divination Bones             | Animal bones/shells thrown to interpret ancestral messages.                     |
| Imbiza             | Clay Pot                     | Holds *muthi* (herbal medicine) or water for rituals.                           |
| Amabhai            | Ritual Mixtures              | Frothy herbal-water blends consumed for ancestral connection.                   |
| Indaba             | Ritual Knife                 | Harvests herbs or performs symbolic cuts.                                       |

---

### **3. Sangoma Rituals**  
| **Zulu Term**        | **English Translation**      | **Description**                                                                 |
|-----------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| ukugeza              | Cleansing                    | Spiritual purification using water, herbs, or smoke.                           |
| Ukulahla Izinkalo     | Throwing the Bones           | Divination practice to diagnose problems or receive guidance.                   |
| Ukubingelela          | Animal Sacrifice             | Offering (e.g., sheep) to ancestors to resolve conflict or seek favor.          |
| Ukucela Imvume        | Asking Permission            | Ritual to request ancestral consent before collecting herbs/water.              |
| Ukubhula              | Calling Ancestors            | Chanting/singing to invoke ancestral presence.                                  |

---

### **4. Sangoma Roles & Titles**  
| **Zulu Term**     | **English Translation**      | **Description**                                                                 |
|--------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| iSangoma           | Traditional Healer           | Diagnoses spiritual/physical ailments using divination and herbs.               |
| iNyanga            | Herbalist                    | Specializes in *muthi* (medicine) for physical healing.                         |
| uMthandazi         | Prayer Healer                | Uses water, prayer, and candles for spiritual work.                             |
| iGobela            | Mentor/Teacher               | Guides initiates (*thwasa*) through training.                                   |

---

### **5. Spiritual Concepts**  
| **Zulu Term**       | **English Translation**      | **Description**                                                                 |
|----------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Amadlozi             | Ancestors                    | Spirits of deceased relatives who guide/protect the living.                    |
| uMamlambo            | River Serpent Spirit         | Water spirit linked to wealth and protection.                                  |
| uMvelinqangi         | Creator/Great Spirit         | Supreme deity in Zulu cosmology.                                               |
| Idlozi               | Personal Ancestor            | Specific ancestor guiding a healer.                                            |

### **7. Medicinal Terms**  
| **Zulu Term**       | **English Translation**      | **Description**                                                                 |
|----------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Muthi                | Medicine                     | Herbal/spiritual remedies (e.g., *umhlonyane* for purification).                |
| Isihlamba            | Protection Paste             | Mixture of herbs/clay applied to the body/home for safety.                      |
| Amafutha Amadlozi    | Ancestral Oil                | Infused oils used to anoint tools/clients during rituals.                       |
| Umhlonyane           | Wild Garlic                  | Burned or brewed for cleansing and ancestral communication.                     |

### **8. Ethical Terms**  
| **Zulu Term**       | **English Translation**      | **Description**                                                                 |
|----------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Ubuntu               | Humanity/Community           | "A person is a person through others"; prioritizes collective well-being.       |
| Izila                | Taboos                       | Forbidden acts (e.g., harming others with *muthi*).                            |
| Ukuhlonipha          | Respect                      | Protocol for honoring ancestors, elders, and sacred spaces.                    |

### **9. Zulu Ritual Phrases**  
| **Zulu Phrase**                | **English Translation**                  | **Context**                                                                     |
|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| *"Baba, ngiyacela izindaba"*   | "Father, I request guidance"             | Spoken during divination to seek ancestral messages.                           |
| *"Ngiyabonga, madlozi"*        | "Thank you, ancestors"                   | Gratitude after a successful ritual.                                           |
| *"Hlala phansi, thokoza"*      | "Sit down, be calm"                      | Grounds a client during healing.                                               |
